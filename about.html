About Me

As an experienced Art Director and Graphic Designer, I specialize in digital oil painting, visual storytelling, and expressive design. Over the years, I’ve collaborated with diverse clients, bringing ideas to life across both digital and print platforms. My work is rooted in artistic expressionism, often inspired by my personal experiences, and executed with a deep knowledge of Adobe Creative Suite and industry-standard tools.

For the past four years, I’ve had the privilege of working with a Vienna-based advertising company that focuses on animation and motion graphics. This experience has enriched my creative approach, allowing me to merge fine art sensibilities with commercial design strategies in dynamic, engaging ways.

I thrive in creative environments that value originality, concept-driven design, and the power of visual impact.
