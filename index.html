THE ARTIST

About Me

I am Parisa, a visual artist creating original paintings inspired by emotion, memory, people, nature, and places.

My work often explores the connection between inner feelings and the world around us. I am drawn to movement, light, human relationships, city scenes, oceans, and quiet moments that carry a deeper emotional presence.

A Story Behind Every Painting

Each painting is created as an individual story. Through color, texture, and form, I try to capture moments that may feel familiar, personal, or open to interpretation.

My goal is not only to create something visually beautiful, but also to make work that creates a feeling and stays with the viewer.
